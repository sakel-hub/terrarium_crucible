## How Terrarium Crucible Brings the Jam Themes to Life

*Terrarium Crucible* was crafted around the jam's spirit—**"A little magic, a little game"**—weaving together three themes from the Ingredient Pantry:

1. **One small world ("Build a whole adventure in a tiny place"):**
   Your entire adventure takes place inside an open-top glass terrarium sitting on a wizard's wooden desk. You begin in a single cozy glass room caring for one magical sprout. Each time your garden blooms to `100%`, you pick a permanent upgrade and the glass walls open outward along a spiral—adding a new connected room, another plant growing at its own natural pace, and shimmering **Prismatic Geode** bonus crystals.

2. **Finding balance ("Keep opposing forces in harmony"):**
   The heart of the game is keeping your tiny ecosystem comfortable for your plants. As the wizard drops fiery **Brimstone**, freezing **Ice Shards**, sour **Acid Slime**, and dry **Chalk** in from above, you balance warmth and soil health—catching blocks mid-air, melting ice into water for your flask, dusting away acid to pave green speed paths, or picking up opposite blocks with your trowel so they cancel each other out.

3. **A change of perspective ("See a familiar world in a new way"):**
   Instead of exploring a giant landscape at human scale, you are shrunk down to the size of a thimble. Peering out through the brass-framed glass walls reveals towering wooden desk planks, giant beeswax candles flickering warmly in the dark, and the open pages of the wizard's spellbook.
