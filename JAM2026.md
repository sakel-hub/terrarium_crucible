## How Terrarium Crucible Brings the Jam Themes to Life

*Terrarium Crucible* was crafted around the jam's spirit—**"A little magic, a little game"**—weaving together three themes from the Ingredient Pantry:

1. **One small world ("Build a whole adventure in a tiny place"):**
   Your entire adventure unfolds inside an open-top glass terrarium sitting on a wizard's wooden desk. You begin in one cozy glass room caring for a single magical sprout. Each time your garden blooms to `100%`, you choose **1 of 3 Next-Level Blessings** and the brass-framed glass walls open outward along a spiral—adding a new connected room, another plant growing at its own natural rhythm, and shimmering **Prismatic Geode** bonus crystals.

2. **Finding balance ("Keep opposing forces in harmony"):**
   The heart of the game is keeping your tiny ecosystem comfortable so your plants can thrive. As the wizard drops fiery **Brimstone**, frosty **Ice Shards**, sour **Acid Slime**, and dry **Chalk** in from above, you balance warmth and soil health—catching blocks mid-air, melting ice into water, sweeping away acid to pave glowing green speed paths, or scooping opposite blocks onto your trowel so they cancel each other out.

3. **A change of perspective ("See a familiar world in a new way"):**
   Instead of crossing a sprawling world at human scale, you are shrunk down to the size of a thimble. Peering out through the glass walls reveals giant wooden desk planks, towering beeswax candles flickering warmly in the night, and the open pages of the wizard's spellbook beneath a glowing crescent moon.

## Crafted for Luanti 5.18

*Terrarium Crucible* embraces Luanti 5.18's newest capabilities to make the workshop feel tactile and welcoming—featuring illustrated book-style menus with glowing slot highlights and vertical book-spine titles, copyable run summaries to share with friends, a live hotbar tongs preview when carrying blocks, mood-reactive candlelight and night-sky atmosphere, crisp multiplayer synchronization with zero chat clutter, and full translations across **9 languages** (`en`, `es`, `fr`, `de`, `pt_BR`, `ru`, `zh_CN`, `ja`, `sk`).
