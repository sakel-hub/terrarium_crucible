## Welcome to the Glass Terrarium

You have been shrunk down to the size of a thimble and placed inside an open-top glass box on a wizard's giant wooden desk, surrounded by tall flickering beeswax candles and an open spellbook.

Inside the glass grows a delicate magical plant called a **Crucible Sprout**. Every few seconds, the wizard drops wild ingredients in from above—blazing hot stone, freezing ice, sour green slime, and dry chalk. Your goal—playing solo or together with friends—is to keep the room comfortable and clean so your plants can grow to **100% Bloom**!

- **Start Small in Level 1**: Care for a single sprout inside one cozy glass room while you learn your tools.
- **Grow Your Garden in Level 2+**: Each time your plants reach **100% Bloom**, you choose **1 of 3 permanent upgrades** and the glass walls open outward to add a brand-new connected room and another sprout—with every plant growing at its own natural pace.
- **Play at Your Own Speed**: Switch between **Casual** (relaxed), **Normal** (balanced), and **Panic** (fast) anytime inside the game by right-clicking the **Control Altar**!

## How to Play in 5 Easy Steps

1. **Watch for Glowing Floor Circles**:
   - Before anything lands, a glowing ring appears on the soil for nearly three seconds and a chime plays so you always see where the next block will fall.
2. **Clear Falling Blocks with Your Tools (`[1]`–`[4]`)**:
   - **Use the matching tool on the floor**: Melt **Ice** with your **Bellows `[2]`**, splash **Brimstone** or **Chalk** with your **Water Flask `[3]`**, and dust green **Acid Slime** with your **Pestle `[4]`**.
   - **Catch blocks in mid-air for extra growth**: Run to the glowing floor circle and **Left-Click** the falling block right before it touches down using its matching tool for an extra-large boost to plant growth!
   - **Combine opposite blocks with your Trowel `[1]`**: **Right-Click** a block on the floor with your **Trowel `[1]`** to pick it up, then **Right-Click** on the floor right next to its opposite (**Brimstone + Ice** or **Acid Slime + Chalk**) so both vanish together in a big burst of growth!
3. **Refill Your Water Flask**:
   - Melting **Ice** with your **Bellows `[2]`** (or combining Brimstone + Ice) leaves behind fresh **Water** and rising **Steam**.
   - **Left-Click** any Water puddle or Steam cloud with your **Water Flask `[3]`** to scoop up the puddle cleanly and fill **3 splashes** at once (`5` splashes if you pick the *Deep Retort* upgrade).
4. **Build Green Speed Paths**:
   - Cleaning up **Acid Slime** or **Chalk** turns brown dirt into glowing green **Fertile Soil**. Plants in rooms with Fertile Soil bloom faster, and walking on it makes you run much faster between rooms!
5. **Keep Heat & Soil Balanced Until 100% Bloom**:
   - Watch the gauges on the right side of your screen: keep **Heat** in the comfortable middle (`40°C – 60°C`), keep **Soil pH** balanced (`5.6 – 8.4 pH`), and don't leave lots of leftover blocks lying around. When everything stays calm, your plants bloom steadily and heal themselves!

| **Glacial Ice Shard** *(Frosty blue crystal)* | Makes the terrarium **too cold**, makes the floor slippery, and chills green Fertile Soil over time. | Melt it with your **Bellows `[2]`** to turn it into **Water** & **Steam**, then **Left-Click** the Water with your **Water Flask `[3]`** to refill! |

| **Alkaline Chalk** *(Pale crystal block)* | Makes the soil **too dry & chalky (alkaline)**. Automatically dissolves if flowing water reaches it. | Splash it with your **Water Flask `[3]`** or **Right-Click** with **Trowel `[1]`** to set it beside **Acid Slime**—both turn the floor into green **Fertile Soil**! |

## Caring for Your Plants, Potions & Upgrades

### Keeping Every Plant Happy
- **Natural Growth**: When Heat (`40°C – 60°C`) and Soil pH (`5.6 – 8.4`) are both in their comfort zones and the floor isn't cluttered, your plants sparkle and grow through **4 visual stages**—from a tiny *Seedling* to Vines, an *Astral Rose*, and finally a glowing *Philosopher's Lotus*. In multi-room levels, each plant grows at its own natural rhythm!
- ** Overdrive Bonus**: Keep the terrarium in calm balance for **8 seconds in a row** and everyone gets a **Harmony Boost**—faster running, gentle health recovery, and wider tool reach!
- **Healing & Reviving Wilted Plants**:
  - If a room stays too hot, too cold, too sour, or crowded with hazards right next to a sprout, that plant loses **Plant Health**. If a plant drops low, an on-screen warning marker points right to its room—even through walls!
  - At `0%` health, a plant **wilts** and pauses its growth, but **you haven't lost yet** as long as at least one other plant is still alive (or before it wilts in Level 1). Revive any wilted plant right away in **3 easy ways**:
    1. **Left-Click** the wilted plant with a filled **Water Flask `[3]`**.
    2. Drink an **Aegis Potion** anywhere in the terrarium.
    3. Keep the terrarium in calm **Harmony** for **6 seconds** (`4` seconds with the *Ley-Line Roots* upgrade).

### Helpful Potions (Earned Automatically as You Play!)
- **Quicksilver Potion** *(Earned every 3 hazards cleared)*: Drink with Left-Click or Right-Click for **15 seconds** of super-fast running and higher jumps.
- **Aegis Potion** *(Earned every 5 hazards cleared)*: Restores your **full health**, revives **every wilted plant**, and shields all plants from harm for **12 seconds**. Even better: if you ever take a knockout hit while carrying one in your bag, it triggers **automatically** to save you!
- **Chronos Hourglass** *(Earned every time you finish a level)*: Use it to **pause new falling blocks for 12 seconds**, clear a hazard near every plant, and speed up the whole team so you can catch your breath.

### Permanent Upgrades Between Levels
Every time your plants reach **100% Bloom** (with all plants healthy and awake), you pick **1 of 3 permanent upgrades** for the rest of your run:
- **Deep Retort**: Your **Water Flask `[3]`** holds **5 splashes** per refill instead of `3`.
- **Dragonlung Bellows**: Your **Thermal Bellows `[2]`** melts a wide patch of ice at once instead of a single block.
- **Starlight Mortar**: Your **Limestone Pestle `[4]`** clears an even wider patch of green acid and gives extra plant growth.
- **Ley-Line Roots**: Plants heal faster during calm balance and wilted plants revive automatically in **4 seconds** instead of `6`.
- **Hermetic Boots**: You run even faster on green **Fertile Soil** and take **half damage** from hot Brimstone and Acid puddles.

## How You Win & How a Run Can End

- **Winning a Level**: Fill **Bloom to `100%`** with **all plants awake** (if one plant is wilted, Bloom waits at `99%` until you revive it!). Pick your upgrade and watch the terrarium open a new room!
- **Three Ways a Run Can End**:
  1. **Every Plant Wilts at Once**: Keep at least one plant healthy while you revive any wilted ones with your **Water Flask `[3]`** or an **Aegis Potion**.
  2. **Too Many Uncleared Blocks (8-Second Warning)**: If too many hazard blocks pile up across the floor (`11` in Level 1, growing by `+5` per room), an **8-second Overload countdown** flashes on screen. Clear a few blocks before the `8` seconds finish and the danger stops right away!
  3. **Running Out of Health**: Standing on fiery Brimstone or green Acid hurts your character. In solo play, running out of health brings you back at the Altar while shaking `30%` health off your plants (only ending the run if that wilts your last plant). In co-op, the run only ends if the entire team is knocked down at the same time.
