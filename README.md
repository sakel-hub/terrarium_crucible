## Welcome to the Glass Terrarium

You have been shrunk down to the size of a thimble and placed inside an open-top glass terrarium on a wizard's giant wooden desk, surrounded by tall flickering beeswax candles and an open spellbook under a moonlit night sky.

Inside the glass grows a delicate magical plant called a **Crucible Sprout**. Every few seconds, the wizard drops wild ingredients in from above—fiery stone, frosty ice, sour green slime, and dry chalk. Playing solo or together with friends, your job is to keep each glass room comfortable and clean so your plants can grow all the way to **100% Bloom**!

- **Start Small in Level 1**: Care for a single sprout inside one cozy glass room—with all your tools charged and ready—while you get comfortable with the rhythm.
- **Open New Glass Rooms in Level 2+**: Each time your plants reach **100% Bloom**, you choose **1 of 3 Next-Level Blessings** to power you through the next stage, and brass-framed archways open outward into a brand-new connected room with another sprout. From Level 3 onward, expect special **Wizard's Trial Waves** and an exciting **85% Final Bloom Surge**!
- **Play at Your Own Pace**: Switch freely between **Casual** (relaxed), **Normal** (balanced), and **Panic** (fast) anytime during a run by **Right-Clicking any Plant** (or opening your Inventory `[I]`)!

## How to Play in 5 Easy Steps

1. **Watch for Glowing Floor Circles**:
   - Before anything lands, a colored ring glows on the soil for nearly three seconds and a gentle chime plays—and a **Next Drop** preview card in the top-right corner always tells you what is coming next.
2. **Clear Falling Blocks with Your Tools (`[1]`–`[4]`)**:
   - **Use the matching tool on the floor**: Melt **Ice** with your **Bellows `[2]`**, splash **Brimstone** or **Chalk** with your **Water Flask `[3]`**, and sweep green **Acid Slime** with your **Pestle `[4]`**.
   - **Swat blocks in mid-air for extra growth**: Step up to a glowing landing circle and **Left-Click** the falling crystal right before it touches down using its matching tool—or ride rising **Steam** into the air for a big **Aerial Catch** bonus!
   - **Combine opposite blocks with your Trowel `[1]`**: **Left-Click** or **Right-Click** any block on the floor with your **Trowel `[1]`** to scoop it onto your tongs, then click onto (or right beside) its opposite (**Brimstone + Ice** or **Acid Slime + Chalk**) so both cancel out in a bright burst of plant growth!
3. **Keep Your Tools Topped Up**:
   - Every tool starts Level 1 fully charged (`4/4` **Bellows**, `3/3` **Water Flask**, `4/4` **Pestle**) and automatically gains **`+1` extra max capacity** on Levels 3, 5, and 7.
   - Refill right from the garden: scoop **Water** or **Steam** with your **Water Flask `[3]`**, draw heat from **Brimstone** when your **Bellows `[2]`** runs low (`≤1` puff), or grind **Chalk** when your **Pestle `[4]`** runs low (`≤1` sweep).
   - Cracking a rainbow **Prismatic Geode** refills **all three tools at once**—and if a tool ever hits `0` with nothing nearby, simply **Left-Click the open air** for a quick `+1` backup charge so you can never get stuck!
4. **Pave Green Speed Paths**:
   - Cleaning up **Acid Slime** or **Chalk** turns ordinary brown dirt into glowing green **Fertile Soil**. Rooms with Fertile Soil help plants bloom faster, and walking on it lets you dash quickly from room to room!
5. **Keep Warmth & Soil Balanced Until 100% Bloom**:
   - Glance at the gauges on the right side of your screen: keep **Heat** in the cozy middle (`40°C – 60°C`), keep **Soil pH** balanced (`5.6 – 8.4`), and clear leftover blocks before the floor gets crowded. Active potion shields and speed/jump boosts show clear countdown bars right below your gauges.

## Caring for Your Plants, Potions & Blessings

### Keeping Every Plant Happy
- **Four Stages of Growth**: When Heat (`40°C – 60°C`) and Soil pH (`5.6 – 8.4`) stay in their comfort zones and the floor stays tidy, your sprouts grow through **4 stages**—from a *Seedling* to *Vines*, an *Astral Rose*, and finally a glowing *Philosopher's Lotus*.
- **Harmony Boost**: Hold the terrarium in calm balance for **8 seconds** in a row to trigger a **Harmony Boost**—granting faster movement, gentle health recovery, and wider tool reach!
- **Reviving Wilted Plants**:
  - If a room stays uncomfortable or hazards crowd around a plant, it loses **Plant HP** and raises a warning marker you can see through walls.
  - At `0%` health, a plant **wilts** and pauses its growth, **but the run keeps going** as long as at least one other plant is still awake! Revive any wilted plant in **3 ways**:
    1. **Left-Click** it with a filled **Water Flask `[3]`**.
    2. Drink an **Aegis Potion** anywhere in the garden.
    3. Hold the terrarium in calm **Harmony** for **6 seconds** (`4` seconds with *Ley-Line Roots*).

### Lifesaver Potions & Speed Paths
- **Fertile Speed Soil**: Stepping onto glowing green soil boosts your movement speed (`1.50×`, or `1.70×` with *Hermetic Boots*) for `3.5s` so you can glide effortlessly across multi-room gardens.
- **Aegis Potion** *(Earned on Level Complete & from Prismatic Geodes)*: Restores **full health**, revives **every wilted plant**, and wraps **you and all plants** in a protective shield for **12 seconds** (`14s` with *Sunlit Dew*). Carrying one in your belt also saves you **automatically** if you ever take a knockout hit!
- **Chronos Hourglass** *(Earned on Level Complete)*: Freezes incoming block drops for **12 seconds**, clears a hazard near every plant, and gives the whole team a crisp speed boost so you can tidy up the garden in peace.

### Next-Level Blessings (A Fresh Choice Every Level!)
Finishing a level lets you pick **1 of 3 Next-Level Blessings** from 8 magical perks that empower you for the entire upcoming level:
- **Deep Retort**: Your **Water Flask `[3]`** holds **`+2` extra splashes** per refill.
- **Dragonlung Bellows**: Your **Thermal Bellows `[2]`** holds **`+2` extra puffs**, melts a **`3×3` patch** of ice at once, and grants bonus Bloom.
- **Starlight Mortar**: Your **Limestone Pestle `[4]`** holds **`+2` extra sweeps**, clears a **`5×5` patch** of acid at once, and grants bonus Bloom.
- **Ley-Line Roots**: Plants recover health much faster during calm balance and wilted plants revive on their own in **4 seconds** instead of `6`.
- **Hermetic Boots**: Run even faster on green **Fertile Soil** (`1.70×`) and take **half damage** from hot stone, acid puddles, and falling blocks.
- **Featherfall Winds**: Falling blocks drift down gently, waves pause `+1.5` extra seconds between drops, and mid-air catches grant bigger Bloom bonuses.
- **Crystal Resonance**: Rainbow **Prismatic Geodes** appear **twice as often** (every 2nd wave) and Trowel combos grant `+25%` extra Bloom.
- **Sunlit Dew**: Begin the level with `+1` extra **Aegis Potion** (`14s` shield, Belt Cap `3`) and heal **`1 Heart` every `3s`** during calm balance!

## Winning, Hazards & Top 10 Scores

- **Finishing a Level**: Reach **100% Bloom** while **every plant is awake** (if one plant has wilted, Bloom waits at `99%` until you revive it!).
- **How a Run Can End**:
  1. **Every Plant Wilts at Once**: Keep at least one plant awake while reviving wilted ones.
  2. **Floor Overload (8-Second Grace Timer)**: If too many uncleared blocks pile up (`11` in Level 1, plus `5` for each new room), an **8-second warning** counts down—clear a few blocks in time and you're safe!
  3. **Knockout**: In solo play, running out of health brings you back in Room 1 while shaking `30%` health off your plants. In co-op, the run only ends if everyone is knocked down at once.
- **Top 10 Leaderboard & Shareable Summaries**: View the **Top 10 Scores** anytime from a plant, your Inventory `[I]`, or `/leaderboard`—complete with a one-click copyable run summary to share with friends!
